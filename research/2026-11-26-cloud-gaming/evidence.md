## Publication limits

No account was created or used, no terms were accepted, and no purchase, queue, game session, balance credit, cloud latency or character-progress transfer was tested. A click on the public support navigation produced a terms/login prerequisite; it was not continued. Do not imply that the cloud and native versions, all channels or regional accounts share progress based on this notice alone.

The cited notice does not establish a Japan cloud service, English client, global English service, native macOS package, Steam or console release. It does not give an exact cloud app size, bitrate, frame rate or measured latency. Its performance wording is a publisher description, not our test result. It also does not fully specify time deductions while queuing/disconnected, free/paid consumption order, grant reset clock, free balance expiry, complete prices, play-card activation/stacking or refund handling. Do not infer any of these.

The 590 + 15 capped at 600 example in the guides is explicitly arithmetic under an unchanged balance assumption. It is not an observation of account billing. The product upgrade's 21-day entitlement is kept separate from the direct purchase's 42 days.
