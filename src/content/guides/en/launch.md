## Azur Promilia is live in Japan and mainland China

**Japan and mainland China officially began service on November 26, 2026. If you are starting now, choose the correct regional client, check your account before creating a character, finish the current tutorial and separate immediate mail from rewards that require more progress.** An opening announcement does not guarantee that every server is available during later maintenance.

This page helps you get through your first session. For Korea, the Traditional Chinese edition, PlayPark SEA and the future English release, use the [regional release comparison](/en/guides/global-release/). Their publisher, language and account rules should not be inferred from this Japan–China checklist.

## Opening times and current login status

The original openings were **November 26 at 10:00 UTC+8 for China** and **11:00 JST for Japan**, both **02:00 UTC**. Service-start notices from [China](https://azurpromilia.manjuu.com/home/news/detail/912762865511960576) and [Japan](https://azurpromilia.jp/news/5225) confirm that launch occurred. Those historical times are not a live server monitor.

If you cannot enter today, read the current notice for your edition. Separate a scheduled maintenance or queue from an account prompt and a failure while loading extra resources. A completed download alone does not identify which of those is stopping you.

## Before creating your first character

1. Confirm the publisher and region on the client you installed.
2. Use the account intended to receive any earned web-event rewards.
3. Check available space on the actual installation drive and allow for additional resources.
4. Follow the current tutorial to unlock features instead of relying on an unverified timed route.
5. Check mail, login rewards and event conditions before spending tickets.

Japan’s nest event uses the participating Yostar ID’s first created character. China’s nest event has its own first-character condition under the participating Manjuu account. If you paid in China’s historical test, the original paying account also matters for [test-spending returns](/en/guides/recharge-return/). These are reasons to check the identity before repeatedly restarting.

## Already preloaded? What happens next?

Preloading began on November 24: China’s announced time was 15:00 UTC+8, while Japan’s was 16:00 JST. These are completed preparation milestones. Japan’s FAQ says a preloaded client needs additional resources for release; that does not mean every preload user must download the whole game again.

For a missing installer, repeated resource downloads or an old China test client, use [official downloads and installation help](/en/guides/download/). The regional recommendations of **120GB free on PC** and **55GB on mobile** are storage guidance rather than measured payload sizes. Read [device requirements](/en/guides/requirements/) before treating a storage number as RAM or an installer size.

## First-session rewards: mail, codes and web events

| Reward route | What to do | Common mix-up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Preregistration or thank-you mail | Read the current regional distribution conditions | Assuming everything uses a redeem code |
| Login or progression benefits | Check remaining days, quests or levels | Counting unfinished rewards as already owned |
| Nest web event | Match participating account, earned items and receiving character | Assuming the preregistration gift has the same conditions |
| Japan code AZPR1126 | Use 設定 → アカウント → 引き換えコード | Applying a Japan code to another regional edition |

Japan officially announced **AZPR1126**, with a deadline of **November 26, 2027, 23:59**. The original post does not separately state a time zone. Its mail-delivered thank-you gift is a different route and does not require that code. Follow [redeem-code instructions](/en/guides/redeem-codes/) and [regional reward details](/en/guides/resources/) for the exact source and conditions.

## Before your first pulls and upgrades

The beginner banner’s ten observations cost eight ordinary tickets in the independently cited China and Japan rules. Three ten-pulls cost 24 tickets and count as 30 observations; this is not a selectable five-star. Limited-character and limited-anima histories are separate. See [pity and banners](/en/guides/banner-rules/) before combining ticket and observation totals.

For a China standard-selector decision, use the [reroll guide](/en/guides/reroll/) to compare missing teammates against your roster. Once you have a workable team, continue to [combat](/en/systems/combat/), [farm production](/en/systems/home/) or [Kibo capture](/en/systems/kibo/) according to what you encounter in the tutorial. Exact unlock levels and a measured fastest first-pull route remain unverified.

## If your first session is blocked

Record the exact error, regional edition, device, client version and attempt time, then use [official support](/en/guides/support/). Include whether the failure happened before login, during resource loading or after entering the game. That distinction is more useful than treating every launch-period problem as the same server outage.
