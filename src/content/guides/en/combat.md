## Build one usable team, then solve the fight’s main problem

**Start with a team your current roster can support. If a fight goes wrong, distinguish a skill that never activates, an attack that misses, a character dying quickly and damage that feels low.** Each problem calls for a different change. Spending more upgrade materials before identifying it can leave the original obstacle untouched.

The team examples below are mainland-China community suggestions. They are not tested rankings, official English character names or proof that the same combinations behave identically in another regional client. Keep current in-game descriptions open when comparing a teammate, anima or Kibo.

## China starter team examples

[带侠虎’s November 27 community guide](https://news.17173.com/content/11272026/112816971.shtml) proposes three early combinations. Its images carry a test-version watermark, so the skill interactions discussed by the author should not be treated as independently verified launch mechanics.

| Proposed team | Author’s idea | Before investing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 末音 + 星临者 + 米蒂 | A core using one five-star, the protagonist and a four-star teammate | Check that you actually own both supporting characters |
| 红宝石 + 法兰塔 + 米蒂 or 星临者 | The author discusses lightning marks supporting direct damage | 米蒂 and 星临者 are alternatives for the third slot, not two compulsory extra members |
| 夏儿 + 阿比 + 璐璐卡 | The author assigns damage, breaking and support roles | All three are permanent five-stars; account for missing members |

For someone who owns only 夏儿, the article also mentions 卡塔露 and 艾妮丝 as four-star transition candidates. A transition option does not promise identical effects. The spelling 夏儿 follows the [official China standard roster](/en/characters/), rather than the article’s 夏尔 transcription. These Chinese names are retained because confirmed official English names are not available in the cited evidence.

If you are deciding which missing teammate to select, use the [reroll and selector guide](/en/guides/reroll/). These examples cannot determine a dedicated anima requirement, exact Kibo pairing, constellation threshold or optimal skill-upgrade order. There is no controlled same-level, same-equipment damage comparison behind this table.

## Why does a skill not activate?

Read the active tutorial objective and skill conditions first. Then check input settings, target position, available resources and cooldown. This is a troubleshooting order, not a claim that every character uses the same resource or targeting rule.

| What you see | Check first | What to try next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A tutorial step does not advance | The requested action and designated target | Return to the required scene and follow the on-screen prompt |
| A key produces no response | Current bindings and whether the game window has input focus | Compare another bound action; distinguish missing input from a frozen frame |
| An animation plays but nothing is hit | Target position, distance and area indicators | Change your position and watch the hit feedback |
| A skill works only sometimes | Resource, cooldown and status requirements in its description | Compare the visible conditions before each attempt |
| Combat stutters or the client exits | Device performance and the exact error | Follow the [requirements and crash checks](/en/guides/requirements/) |

Changing one condition at a time makes the result easier to interpret. A successful animation does not automatically prove the intended target was affected. Likewise, a skill on cooldown is not evidence of a broken key binding.

## Damage or survival: what should you improve first?

If your character falls quickly, examine available healing, defensive effects and avoidance options before committing everything to damage. Read the replacement character’s actual skills: sharing a broad support label does not make two characters interchangeable. First aim to stay alive long enough to perform the team’s intended actions.

If survival is stable but progress is slow, compare the main damage dealer’s skills with the equipment and teammates you selected. Check whether bonuses serve the same damage method and whether triggers can occur in that encounter. Avoid choosing an attribute solely from a role label or spending on three separate team plans at once.

## Where are elemental reactions and multipliers listed?

Use your current client’s tutorials and descriptions for the skill’s target, trigger, cost, duration and cooldown. This guide does not yet contain a verified complete launch reaction table or damage formula. A character’s story profile or the color of an animation cannot establish one.

For companion roles, continue to [Kibo capture and uses](/en/systems/kibo/). If behavior still appears abnormal after checking the visible conditions, record the character, encounter, attempted input, result, regional version and device. A screenshot showing the skill prompt can help [official support](/en/guides/support/) understand the problem without repeated spending of scarce materials.
