## Before spending another Kibo capture card

**Read the current target’s capture prompt, the card’s description and any success information shown in your client. For an egg, check whether its preview fixes the species or leaves a random result.** A high success chance in an early area does not establish the same chance for later targets, and one rare egg result cannot prove a guaranteed trait.

This guide separates official regional reward rules from Korean community experiences. It does not contain verified capture coordinates, a complete species map or trait probabilities. Original names identify items in the cited edition; they do not establish official English names or equivalence between different regional names.

## Wild capture: when repeated attempts cost too much

In a [November 27 Korean post](https://arca.live/b/azurpromilia/190482512), **HoYo호두** warns against assuming later captures are as easy as those in the early area. The author points to connection strength, account growth and knowledge nodes as things to examine when chances fall. These are attributed suggestions, not confirmed numeric thresholds or a China upgrade order.

Before a costly attempt, compare the target you want, the current prompt and how many cards you are comfortable using. If repeated attempts are draining a scarce stock, stop and read the relevant card and growth descriptions. That is a budget decision, not a claim that a fixed number of failed attempts changes the next success chance.

| Decision | Read before confirming | Avoid assuming |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A wild target | Current success prompt and target conditions | Early-area success applies to all later species |
| A higher-rarity card | Its actual effects and eligible targets | Color alone proves a universal guarantee |
| An egg | Species preview and stated random elements | Every egg has the same possible results |
| A duplicate companion | Current work capabilities, traits and disposal rules | A duplicate is automatically useless |

Use your regional in-game encyclopedia’s source hints when looking for a species. Artwork or an official character introduction does not provide a capture location. The [Kibo name and artwork catalog](/en/kibo/) helps identify published companions; it is not a completed location database.

## Egg cards and the Korean 별빛 report

**리트리버’s** [Korean egg-card post](https://arca.live/b/azurpromilia/190520332) reports using **환상의 스타링크** on an egg and obtaining the **별빛** trait. A follow-up clarification says it was an egg with a specified target species. The author was unsure where the card came from.

That single result does not establish a guaranteed trait, a probability or the behavior of every egg. It also does not prove that a similarly colored China card is the same item. Check your egg preview and the exact card wording before using a rare item, especially if your goal is a particular species rather than simply opening any egg.

## Official event Kibo: China and Japan names

The official reward lists describe several different routes. Keep them separate even when an illustration looks similar or an English community nickname is shared.

| Edition and source | Original reward name | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| China preregistration / social rewards | 星彩·浮云 | The regional distribution notice and mail conditions |
| China login reward | 星彩·耶罗达 | The required login progress, rather than assuming immediate delivery |
| China nest exploration | 星彩·小星喵 | Participating Manjuu account and its first created character |
| Japan preregistration / social rewards | 星彩・クロディーメ | The Japan distribution notice and mail |
| Japan nest exploration | 星彩・キラミィ | Participating Yostar ID, earned web items and receiving character |

Japan’s [nest event rules](https://kiboadventure.azurpromilia.jp/) set the event end at **December 31, 2026, 13:00 JST**, and assign earned items to the participating Yostar ID’s first created character. The receipt period is within **365 days of first game login**. This does not automatically apply to Japan’s separate preregistration gift.

China’s nest event ends at **December 31, 2026, 12:00 UTC+8** and separately uses the first character under the participating Manjuu account. Do not move these conditions to another regional publisher. If mail is missing, first compare the exact event, account and reward name using [the rewards guide](/en/guides/resources/) before recreating a character.

## What can Kibo do after you obtain them?

China’s official store overview describes forming **星结** partnerships and working together in exploration, combat and home production. Japan’s store description separately describes Kibo cooperation and farm management. Those overviews establish broad activities; the individual companion’s current abilities determine its jobs.

For production, check the [farm guide](/en/systems/home/) for seeds, assignment suitability and food. For battle problems, use [combat troubleshooting](/en/systems/combat/) to distinguish activation, hit and survival issues before replacing a companion. A shiny appearance alone does not specify every trait, and rarity alone does not establish the best worker for a particular station.

## Should you keep duplicates or spend the rare card now?

Read the companion’s capabilities and the current disposal or conversion prompt before making an irreversible choice. Keeping a duplicate temporarily is reasonable when you have not yet compared its jobs and traits. No verified universal “discard all duplicates” rule or optimal rare-card route is supported here. Match the next purchase or capture to a goal you can actually identify in your current edition.
