## Where should you contact Azur Promilia support?

**For Japan, use the [official FAQ and contact page](https://azurpromilia.jp/contact) or the game's documented support route.** The Japan page accepts inquiries in Japanese and replies to the email address supplied; it does not offer telephone support. This English fan guide cannot recover accounts, move rewards or submit a ticket for you.

For mainland China, follow the local official support instructions and your actual client or distribution channel. An article's display language does not change which publisher operates your account.

## Identify the affected stage before reporting

A completed download, successful installation, server opening and successful account login are different events. Record the stage where one specific attempt stopped.

| Stage | What to check first | Next guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launcher or client download | Official regional URL, installation drive and exact download error | [Downloads](/en/guides/download/) |
| Verification or additional data | Whether the failing program is the launcher or game client | [Regional troubleshooting](/en/guides/download/#download-failed-choose-the-stage-and-region) |
| Login blocked or maintenance message | Latest regional maintenance notice, account, exact error and attempt time | [Release status and regions](/en/guides/global-release/) |
| Account or real-name prompt | Regional account, distribution route and official instructions | The correct regional support below |
| Missing mail or a locked feature | Reward source, character, conditions and current lock message | [Rewards](/en/guides/resources/) |

Check the latest regional notice for maintenance or service interruptions. A temporary service restriction needs a different response from an account or installation error; keep the exact message when contacting support.

## Japan: language, email replies and missing responses

The [Japan contact instructions](https://azurpromilia.jp/contact) explain that replies go only to the email address provided. Use an address you can receive mail at, check spelling and spam folders, and allow messages from **@azurpromilia.jp**.

The page notes that inquiry volume and circumstances may delay an answer. It does not promise a fixed response time. This guide has not sent a ticket or measured turnaround.

An English reader may need to prepare a Japanese description. The template below provides field labels; write your actual observations and do not change unknown details into a claimed diagnosis. Enter account information only where the official form requires it.

## What to record before contacting support

Download the [English troubleshooting record](/downloads/launch-troubleshooting-en.txt), then describe one failure clearly: where you were, what you did, what you expected and what actually appeared.

- Region, platform, client source and current game / launcher versions.
- Device model and OS. On PC, add CPU, GPU, RAM, installation drive and SSD/HDD type when relevant.
- Date, time and time zone. Avoid only writing “just now” or “yesterday.”
- Exact error wording and a screenshot that shows the failing stage.
- Each attempted action and its result, including actions that changed nothing.
- Whether it happened once, repeatedly or every time.

If you cannot log in and have no game UID, say so. A web-event participation ID is not automatically a game-character UID. Keep passwords and verification codes out of screenshots, fan-site issues and general troubleshooting notes.

## Mainland China: official versus channel support

The China [November 24 FAQ](https://azurpromilia.manjuu.com/home/news/detail/912070871064121344/) lists the in-game route **主页面 → 设置 → 账户 → 用户中心 → 联系客服**, with the actual game entry taking precedence. If you cannot enter, use **客服中心** on the [official China site](https://azurpromilia.manjuu.com/).

China's FAQ requires real-name verification before login. If verification fails, contact the account provider; processing times and account-change options are unconfirmed. This requirement comes from the China FAQ. If the problem concerns a channel account, follow that channel's support and client instructions rather than assuming the official-server account can replace it.

For test-payment returns, preserve the original paying account. Do not use account recreation as a general fix for missing entitlements.

## Restart the right program

Japan's FAQ advises restarting the **launcher** for persistent file-verification problems after following the retry instructions. China's corresponding advice names the **client**. Additional-resource failures and crashes have their own instructions; a crash during play starts with restarting the **device** before launching the game again.

Follow the regional steps in the [download guide](/en/guides/download/) and write down which program you restarted. “I restarted it” leaves support unable to distinguish a launcher retry from a device reboot. These are official suggestions, not repairs tested by this site.

## Missing rewards and locked features

For Japan nest rewards, check the participating Yostar ID, earned items in the web bag and the first created character. For preregistration mail, check that program's distribution notice separately. A farming feature with a quest lock is different from a production failure after all stated conditions are met.

Include the lock message or the missing attachment’s name when contacting support. Unlock levels and menu locations vary; check your current quest and the in-game message.

## Support questions

### Can this site contact the publisher for me?

No. The downloadable records stay with you until you choose to send them through the official route. The site's correction link is for guide errors, not game-account support.

### Should I delete my account to fix login or rewards?

Do not treat deletion as a generic repair step. Japan's account terms state that deleted accounts cannot be restored. Follow the official account-specific guidance and preserve existing event and character information.

### What if the issue is an error in this guide?

Use the site's [correction route](/en/#corrections) with the affected guide URL, region, passage and original evidence. For game login or reward problems, continue through regional official support. The [English FAQ](/en/faq/) can help identify the appropriate guide first.

## China download optimization is still in progress

The [November 27 official China notice](https://www.taptap.cn/moment/875477210155189543) acknowledges long iOS download/verification waits with large resources or unstable networks, occasional stalls and repeated login-page verification. Resource packaging, verification and interrupted-download resumption are being optimized through staged platform updates, prioritizing iOS. **This is not a completed-fix announcement.**

If the problem persists, send the failure stage, device, version, error and time to regional support. Avoid repeatedly deleting downloaded resources. The notice covers China; check Japan’s own notices for Japan issues. China’s [official cloud service](/en/guides/cloud-gaming/) is another option if its account and network requirements suit you.
