## Azur Promilia pity: 90 for characters, 80 for anima

**Japan's November 26 launch announcement independently confirms a maximum of 90 observations for the featured limited five-star character and 80 for the featured limited five-star anima. Every five-star on these limited banners is the current featured item, and counts carry to the next banner of the same category.** Mainland China's separate probability disclosures confirm its own 90/80 guarantees and publish base rates and probability increases.

This English guide covers the Japanese and mainland-China services. Original item and banner names help you identify the correct screen; they are not official English localizations. These regional rules do not establish a global English server or English client support. The standard-banner notice was checked again on November 28; the remaining launch sources were checked after service began.

## Japan: current banners and end time

| Japanese category and banner | Featured five-star | Maximum guarantee | End time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 星弦の観測 — 桃花の契り | Character 塗山小玉 | 90 observations; every five-star is featured | December 17, 2026, 04:59 JST |
| 絆心の観測 — 花霞を胸に | Anima 花ノ舞 | 80 observations; every five-star is featured | December 17, 2026, 04:59 JST |
| 始源の観測 — beginner banner | Five-star character | Within 30 observations | After completing its cumulative 30 observations |

The [Japan launch announcement](https://azurpromilia.jp/news/5225) says both limited banners begin with service on November 26. The limited categories carry their counts separately to the next limited character or anima banner. Do not add character and anima histories together or assume a count can move between accounts or regions.

The end time is early morning in Japan, not the evening of December 17. A maximum guarantee is not an average cost. A five-star being featured does not mean every observation produces a five-star. Check your current history and the banner's own details before committing resources.

## Japan beginner banner: eight tickets for ten observations

Japan's **始源の観測** uses **無垢な痕跡 ×8 for ten observations**, with a five-star character guaranteed within 30 observations. Three ten-pulls therefore cost 24 tickets and count as 30 observations. The banner ends after its cumulative 30 are completed.

The observation feature unlocks as you progress. Follow the current quest prompts to unlock it. The beginner guarantee is not a five-star selector. For Japan's base probabilities, probability-increase curve and the complete count-reset wording, consult Japanese in-game details; China's more detailed disclosure cannot fill those gaps automatically.

China's beginner banner allows only ten-pulls, each costing eight 纯粹忆痕. It closes at a cumulative 30 observations and its observation and guarantee counts are independent of other banners. An early five-star does not establish another guaranteed five-star on your next ten-pull.

## China: when the probability increases

The China character disclosure says that after 70 consecutive observations without a five-star, the probability increases by **2.000% each time for observations 71–80**, then **8.000% each time for 81–89**, with a five-star guaranteed at 90. The anima version starts after 60: **61–70 increase by 2.000% each time**, **71–79 by 8.000% each time**, with a guarantee at 80.

These are the disclosure's stated increments. The 70/60 thresholds begin the increased-probability stages; they are not hard guarantees. After obtaining a five-star, the next observation returns to the respective base probability. Limited five-star counts carry within the same category and restart after that featured five-star is obtained. Character, anima, beginner and permanent histories must not be combined.

Japan's launch article does not publish the same curve. Even where the maximum counts match, use each region's own disclosure for the rest of the rules.

## Does a ten-pull guarantee a four-star character?

**China's wording guarantees at least one four-star-or-higher item, and four-star items include both characters and anima.** Both limited categories publish a four-star base rate of 6.000% and an overall rate of 12.000% including guarantees.

Conditional on obtaining a four-star, China's character category gives 50% to the set of boosted four-star characters, 25% to non-boosted characters and 25% to non-boosted anima. The anima category instead gives 50% to boosted four-star anima, with the other two groups at 25% each. That 50% is not the probability on every observation or a guarantee of one specific four-star. The beginner banner lists a four-star overall rate of 10.973%, separate from its 6.000% base.

## First-banner names, carryover and expiry

China's first banners are [桃笺为契](https://azurpromilia.manjuu.com/home/news/detail/912104344520888320), featuring 涂山小玉, and [绯影入怀](https://azurpromilia.manjuu.com/home/news/detail/912105412612984832), featuring 花之舞. Both end **December 17 at 03:59 UTC+8**. This matches Japan's displayed instant, but Japan's period was verified from its own notice.

China’s character banner also boosts four-star characters 枫糖 and 艾妮丝. Its anima banner boosts 折纸寄情, 未来之星 and 一点都不痛, with 折纸寄情 explicitly described as limited. China's disclosures say limited five-stars and their corresponding newly introduced limited four-star anima are not added to the character or anima permanent pools when the event ends. This does not make every boosted four-star a newly limited item. Exchange inventory and purchase limits refresh with the displayed shop countdown; the disclosures do not establish a universal daily reset time.

Pity carryover does not prove that unused tickets last forever. Check the actual ticket expiry, conversion rules, regional account and remaining banner time separately.

## China standard five-star selector: 50, 250 and 450 pulls

**China's first selector requires 50 cumulative standard-character observations; each further 200 observations adds another selection.** That gives cumulative milestones of 50, 250 and 450. The second selection is at 250, not 200, and beginner or limited-banner observations do not count toward it.

| China standard pulls | Selectors earned |
| --- | --- |
| 50 | 1 |
| 250 | 2 |
| 450 | 3 |

The [November 26 official standard disclosure](https://azurpromilia.manjuu.com/home/news/detail/912772234182004736) also states a separate five-star guarantee within 90 consecutive standard observations. **The selector does not mean pull 50 itself produces a five-star.** Obtaining a random five-star resets that probability counter; keep it separate from cumulative selector progress.

Both China's beginner and standard-character banners use 纯粹忆痕, but **sharing a ticket does not mean sharing a counter**. Three beginner ten-pulls cost 24 tickets and produce 30 beginner observations. If you then make 50 standard observations, the combined 80 observations include only 50 toward the standard selector. Do not confuse observation totals with ticket costs, or assume you must finish the beginner banner before using the standard one.

The official five-star pool includes 佩佩 and excludes the separately announced limited character 涂山小玉. Check the [China standard roster](/en/characters/) and the in-game selection screen for eligible characters. These are mainland-China terms; they do not establish the Japanese, SEA or future global service's selector rules.

## How many free limited pulls do launch rewards provide?

Japan's launch notice lists アニス and 無垢な痕跡 ×20 among first-login preregistration benefits. Its promotion of **40 or more character-observation opportunities** also includes repeat logins and designated quest completions. It is not a promise of 40 immediate limited-character pulls.

Sort rewards into claimed items, unfinished conditions, beginner tickets, limited-character tickets, limited-anima tickets and other currency. China's three ticket names above now have official uses, but conversion rates and item expiry still need their own details. Read [launch rewards and the first Japan story event](/en/guides/resources/) before spending, or return to the [first-session checklist](/en/guides/getting-started/). No measured average pull cost is available here.
