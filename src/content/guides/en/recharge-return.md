## China test-spending returns: who can claim?

**Players who paid during mainland China’s 旅迹测试 must use the original paying account to receive the release return through in-game mail after mail unlocks.** This is an in-game currency and voucher return, not a cash refund. It is specific to that China test and does not establish a Japan, SEA or future global program.

The [China release FAQ](https://azurpromilia.manjuu.com/home/news/detail/912070871064121344/) describes the return tiers, account rules and vouchers. Original Chinese item names are retained to help identify the correct mail and inventory items; the English descriptions here are not official currency localizations. No personal spending record has been queried by this site.

## How the 200% / 150% / 125% tiers work

Eligible paid spending converts at **CNY 1 = 10 星虹**, with the return rate applied to each spending band. The higher tier boundary does not reduce the rate on earlier spending. Use the marginal calculation below rather than multiplying the entire balance by the last rate reached.

| Eligible spending band | Return rate on that band | 星虹 per CNY in that band |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First CNY 2,000, including the boundary | 200% | 20 |
| Amount above CNY 2,000 through CNY 5,000 | 150% | 15 |
| Amount above CNY 5,000 | 125% | 12.5 |

If eligible paid spending is **A CNY**, the editorial arithmetic is:

- A ≤ 2,000: **A × 20** 星虹.
- 2,000 < A ≤ 5,000: **40,000 + (A − 2,000) × 15** 星虹.
- A > 5,000: **85,000 + (A − 5,000) × 12.5** 星虹.

| Example eligible spending | Calculated currency return |
| --- | --- |
| CNY 1,000 | 20,000 星虹 |
| CNY 2,000 | 40,000 星虹 |
| CNY 3,000 | 55,000 星虹 |
| CNY 5,000 | 85,000 星虹 |
| CNY 6,000 | 97,500 星虹 |

These examples illustrate the published bands. They are not a lookup of your personal entitlement, and the cited wording does not establish every rounding detail. Do not convert them into a limited-pull total without separately verified currency conversion and banner costs.

## Which purchases count?

The FAQ includes paid 星虹, 飞信订约, 普罗米利亚游记, costumes and bundles in eligible cash spending. Free items, gifts, bonus currency and first-purchase double bonuses are not additional paid cash amounts. Compare actual payment records rather than adding the displayed value of every item received during the test.

If a number differs from your expectation, first compare the paying account, channel and eligible cash total. A missing bonus item in this calculation is different from a payment record being absent. Keep the receipt details for the appropriate support route rather than posting account credentials publicly.

## Same account, channel and iOS rules

China official PC or Android test payers can receive their return on iOS using the original eligible account. The fact that the test did not offer iOS payments does not by itself prevent claiming on iOS at release. This is not a transfer to a newly created account.

For a channel edition, use the matching channel client and account rules. A shared phone number does not guarantee that an official-service account and channel account are interchangeable. Check [platform and account boundaries](/en/guides/platforms/) before changing clients to troubleshoot missing mail.

## What do the returned vouchers do?

**飞信订约凭证** is returned up to a maximum of three. Use each voucher from the bag to activate **30 days plus 300 星虹**. Receiving a voucher in mail is not the same as having activated it.

**豪华游记凭证** and **游记拾遗凭证** depend on the corresponding purchase type during the test. Using one activates the corresponding current **普罗米利亚游记** benefit. The vouchers do not expire before use under the described rules, but the mail containing them still needs to be claimed within its own deadline. Check which current pass you intend to activate before using the item.

## Two separate 365-day conditions

The test-return instructions require registering or creating a character **within 365 days after public launch, including the 365th day**. Separately, return mail has a **365-day claim validity**; read the actual deadline shown on the mail. These are distinct conditions, not permission to add them into a guaranteed 730-day window.

They are also different from Japan’s nest-event receipt period measured from first game login. A deadline belonging to [another launch reward](/en/guides/resources/) should not be reused for test returns. China’s official service began on November 26, 2026; the service-start announcement establishes that launch occurred, not the delivery time of your individual message.

## Where to query a missing return

The China official homepage provides **充值返利查询**, the test-return query entry, for Manjuu accounts. Data may be delayed. Channel players should use the matching channel support rather than assuming the official-account tool covers their records.

If the expected message is absent, check the original account, matching client, mail unlock and query result in that order. Keep payment receipts and character information ready for [official support](/en/guides/support/). Restarting on a fresh account does not move the return; consider that before following any [reroll route](/en/guides/reroll/).
