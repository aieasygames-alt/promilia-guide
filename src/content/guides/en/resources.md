## How do you claim Azur Promilia launch rewards?

**First identify the regional service and reward source, then check the account, receiving character and actual game mail.** Japan preregistration benefits and the Kibo nest web event are separate programs. Completing a web task is not the same as earning an exploration reward, and neither is proof that its mail attachment has been claimed.

This English guide puts Japan's rewards first and describes mainland China separately. Original Japanese and Chinese item names are retained so you can match official notices and client text; they are not claims of official English item names.

## Japan Kibo nest event: account, first character and deadline

Read the rules on the official [Kibo nest exploration event](https://kiboadventure.azurpromilia.jp/) before choosing an account or character. Its announced period is **November 20, 2026 at 21:00 through December 31 at 13:00 JST**.

1. Participate using the Yostar ID you intend to use for Japan's game service.
2. Complete the applicable tasks to obtain exploration attempts.
3. Use an attempt to explore, then confirm the earned items in **探索バッグ**.
4. After service starts, use the same Yostar ID and check the first character created under that ID.
5. Collect the earned exploration rewards through game mail within **365 days of your first game login**.

The event allows at most **9 explorations**, and its rules say all exploration rewards can be obtained within that cap. Task caps are not extra explorations beyond nine. At the announced November 24, 16:00 JST pre-download transition, the preregistration task stops and specified download tasks open; this is not the event's final closing time.

The listed exploration rewards include 星彩・キラミィ ×1; three separate 無垢な痕跡 ×1 rewards; 原初の雫 ×1; 閃耀のステラカード ×10; 星祝ボックス・特級 ×1; 清澄な星脈結晶 ×5; and ユニ ×10,000. The list is not an acquisition order or a promise that the Kibo arrives first.

Real-world prizes and gift cards use a separate draw. An exploration reward does not guarantee those prizes. The event's invitation tasks also do not establish the main game's multiplayer requirements.

## If your reward mail has not arrived

1. Confirm the actual regional service status and current account. A pre-download alone does not trigger game-mail delivery.
2. Identify the exact reward program and its eligibility, receiving character and deadline.
3. For nest rewards, check earned items in the web bag; for preregistration gifts, use that program's current distribution notice.
4. Inspect mail title, attachment, expiry and claimed state when the client makes mail available.
5. If the expected item is still missing, record the discrepancy and contact the [correct regional support](/en/guides/support/).

The Japan FAQ does not promise a fixed delivery delay in minutes. This site has not completed a game-mail claim test and cannot move rewards between accounts.

## Launch reward questions

### Are 星彩・クロディーメ and 星彩・キラミィ the same reward?

No. The former appears in Japan's preregistration / social benefits; the latter is a nest exploration reward. Track the programs separately even if both use game mail.

### Can I recreate a character to receive all rewards again?

That has not been established. The nest event explicitly names the first character under the participating ID. Do not treat character recreation, account deletion or reinstalling as a reward-reset method.

### Can I count these benefits as a monthly income?

No verified recurring budget follows from a launch milestone list. Separate one-time rewards, sign-in days, unfinished quests and any recurring sources with their actual reset rules. Begin with the [first-session checklist](/en/guides/getting-started/) before planning spending.
