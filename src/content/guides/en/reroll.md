## Should you reroll in Azur Promilia?

**Keep an account with useful teammates or bound rewards while you compare your options. China’s standard five-star selector starts at 50 standard observations, then 250 and 450; a new account is not required to earn it.** There is no verified universal guest-account reset, cache-clearing method or timed fastest reroll route here. Japan’s account-deletion terms also say deleted accounts cannot be restored, including accidental deletion.

This guide is for English readers playing the existing regional editions. China’s selector terms are not confirmation of a Japan or future global selector. Character names below remain in Chinese because an official English mapping has not been established. Use the original name and portrait to identify someone in the correct regional roster.

## China standard selector: 50, 250 and 450

The [official standard-character disclosure](https://azurpromilia.manjuu.com/home/news/detail/912772234182004736) awards a first selection at **50 cumulative standard observations**, with another for each additional 200. The separate random five-star guarantee is within 90 consecutive standard observations. A selector milestone does not mean that exact observation produces a random five-star.

| Your standard observations | Cumulative selectors | What stays separate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 50 | 1 | Beginner and limited observations do not count |
| 250 | 2 | The second threshold is not 200 total |
| 450 | 3 | Random five-star pity has its own counter |

Read your current selector screen before spending. Sharing a ticket type does not merge banner histories. Three beginner ten-pulls followed by 50 standard observations give 30 beginner observations and 50 toward the selector, not 80 toward the selector. See the [banner and pity guide](/en/guides/banner-rules/) for category rules.

## Who should you choose with your existing characters?

Start with the characters you already own, then ask which eligible choice completes a usable team. The following candidates come from [带侠虎’s November 27 article on 17173](https://news.17173.com/content/11272026/112816971.shtml). Its imagery contains a test-version watermark. These are attributed team ideas, not this site’s launch skill tests or a controlled tier list.

| Your roster or preferred core | Candidate to compare | Why it may fit, and what to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 星临者 and 米蒂 | 末音 | The author proposes this three-character combination with one five-star; it still requires both teammates |
| 红宝石 or 法兰塔 | The other of those two | The author proposes 红宝石 + 法兰塔 + either 米蒂 or 星临者; the third slot is a choice |
| 夏儿 with 阿比 or 璐璐卡 | The missing teammate | The full proposal needs three permanent five-stars, so compare the remaining cost before committing |
| Only 夏儿 | Keep your selector decision open while using available teammates | The article mentions 卡塔露 and 艾妮丝 as four-star transition candidates; their effects are not guaranteed equivalents |
| A 涂山小玉 account | Compare 米砂 against an available 星临者 with 莉莉 or 枫糖 | These are the author’s different investment ideas, not a mandatory companion rule |

The official roster uses **夏儿**; the community article transcribes it as 夏尔. **佩佩 is in the official permanent five-star pool.** The community claim excluding her conflicts with that disclosure and is not used. A profile announcement for another character, such as 源千代, does not by itself establish that character’s current summon availability. Check [the official China roster names](/en/characters/) and the actual selection screen.

## Beginner guarantee: 24 tickets for 30 observations

China’s beginner banner uses eight **纯粹忆痕** for ten observations. Japan independently confirms eight **無垢な痕跡** for ten. Completing three ten-pulls therefore costs **24 tickets**, counts as 30 observations and guarantees a five-star within that total. It is not a selector or 30 limited-character pulls.

Japan’s first-login preregistration benefit lists 20 ordinary tickets. At the beginner discount, two ten-pulls use 16 and leave four; another four tickets are needed for the third ten-pull. This arithmetic describes that banner’s cost, not every reward you will have on your account. Separate mail already claimed from rewards requiring more logins or quest completion.

## What can you lose by restarting?

Japan’s nest rewards are assigned to the participating Yostar ID’s first created character. China’s nest event separately uses the first character under the participating Manjuu account. Neither promises gift transfers to a new reroll account. China’s historical test-spending return also belongs to the original paying account; see [China test returns](/en/guides/recharge-return/).

Japan’s [terms of service](https://azurpromilia.jp/terms/user) make account deletion irreversible. Deleting an account and reinstalling the client are different operations. Do not assume a new character, another region or the same phone number will recover previously assigned benefits.

## When is a start good enough to keep?

A practical stopping point is an account you enjoy with a workable team, the right reward bindings and resources left for progression. That is an editorial decision rule, not a damage threshold. First fix missing inputs, missed attacks or frequent deaths using the [combat guide](/en/systems/combat/); a replacement five-star cannot automatically solve those problems.

Build one usable team before spreading scarce materials across several proposals. Check current skill conditions and available support, then spend toward the problem actually slowing you down. Dedicated anima requirements, exact upgrade priorities and measured clear-time comparisons are not established by the community article.
