## Farm not producing? Check seeds, jobs and food first

**When home production stops, read the displayed reason before adding more workers. Check seeds or materials, job suitability, food, the production timer and storage or collection in that order.** A full assignment list does not by itself show that every job has what it needs. Start with the product needed for your current order, then solve the shortage affecting that product.

This guide combines the official descriptions of Kibo-assisted farming with attributed China and Korean player reports. English menu glosses are descriptive translations, not confirmed English-client labels. Original terms are included so you can find the matching screen in your own edition.

## China farm menus: a useful first route

In a [November 26 TapTap post](https://www.taptap.cn/moment/875088587471717682), **雅中林阁** describes a first-day level 1–15 plan, including a farming hut, dining table and order board. The author says they did not participate in the beta, and comments correct parts of the post. Treat it as an individual route through the menus, not a fixed unlock-level recipe or optimal layout.

| Original China label | Descriptive English meaning | What to look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 耕作小屋 | Farming hut | The current farming task and assigned work |
| 奇波餐桌 | Kibo dining table | Food stock and feeding options |
| 订单看板 | Order board | Products your current orders request |
| 奇波牧场 | Kibo ranch | The ranch’s own assignment and collection display |
| 优先播种高星种子 | Prioritize higher-star seeds | Whether this priority matches the crop you need |
| 一键添加 | Add in one action | Which food will be added before confirming |

Compare sowing, watering and harvesting assignments with the abilities actually shown for your Kibo. An official store description of cooperative production does not mean every species can perform every job. Keep the [Kibo guide](/en/systems/kibo/) nearby when comparing a companion’s uses.

## Korean seed-management path

**Horol’s** [Korean community post](https://arca.live/b/azurpromilia/190507810) shows this route: home overview → **키보 농장** → bottom-right **씨앗 관리** → **씨앗 창고** and the seed slots. The author says configured seeds are planted in the selected order. Check that the slots contain the seeds you intend to use and that stock remains available.

The author discovering this menu at level 21 does not prove it unlocks at level 21. Their anecdote about spending 15–20 minutes planting wheat manually is not a crop-growth timer. Seed configuration also does not prove that completed products are automatically collected. The menu names belong to the Korean screenshots; match your current client rather than assuming the same text or unlock sequence everywhere.

## Why does assigning more Kibo leave you short of food?

A [November 27 China player report](https://www.taptap.cn/moment/875286112719866850) describes food running short after filling jobs and questions whether extra acceleration or refund passives covered the consumption. It is one account’s experience without controlled configurations. It does not establish a universal food rate or make every high-rarity trait a bad choice.

A practical response is to keep essential jobs running and temporarily reduce work you do not currently need. Check the dining table and stored food, then compare the current order with what each active station produces. If production resumes, add optional work gradually while watching the relevant stocks. This helps identify the bottleneck without assuming every station should run continuously.

## A short production troubleshooting order

1. Read the stopped station’s message rather than guessing from an empty progress bar.
2. Check its seeds, ingredients or other required stock.
3. Compare the assigned Kibo’s displayed capabilities with that job.
4. Check food and the dining table before filling more assignments.
5. Look at the actual timer; do not use a community anecdote as a fixed crop duration.
6. Check storage and whether finished output still needs collection.

These steps are editorial troubleshooting advice. Exact production rates, complete unlock levels and a verified maximum-efficiency layout are not established by the cited reports. If your screen differs, use the current in-game explanation and your region’s support route.

## Japan housing sales and China ticket rewards are separate

Japan’s [launch announcement](https://azurpromilia.jp/news/5225) lists the farm **寮** housing content. 塗山小玉’s **春還る花亭** is listed until **January 5, 2027, 04:59 JST**, while メーツァ’s **翠緑の秘苑** is a permanent sale. Decorations, pajamas and avatar-related content do not establish a production boost or a mandatory farming unlock. Check the item description before spending for an expected efficiency benefit.

China’s launch-benefit images separately list eight ordinary tickets from home-level conditions and eight through the home shop. They are separate qualified sources, not 16 tickets immediately available on entering the home. See [regional launch rewards](/en/guides/resources/) for how to distinguish claimed mail, progression rewards and shop purchases. For your next session, prioritize the order you can complete with existing seeds, food and suitable workers.
