## Can you play Azur Promilia on Mac or in a browser?

**The mainland China publisher announced its official cloud version, 云·蓝色星原, on November 26, 2026, with Android and iOS apps and a computer browser service. Its recommended browsers explicitly include macOS.** Start at the [official China cloud site](https://azurpromilia.manjuu.com/cloudgame), or use its [download page](https://azurpromilia.manjuu.com/cloudgame/download) for the mobile cloud apps.

The [China launch notice](https://azurpromilia.manjuu.com/home/news/detail/912757675975118848/) covers Mac browser play, mobile cloud apps and free time for China’s service. Japan cloud availability and English client support are unconfirmed. The details below come from public notices; we have not tested cloud gameplay or performance.

## Supported cloud devices and recommended browsers

| Device | Method in the announcement | Listed recommendations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Windows computer | Browser service | Current Chrome, Firefox, Edge or a Chromium-based browser |
| Mac / macOS | Browser service | Current Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Edge or a Chromium-based browser |
| iPhone / iPad | iOS cloud app | iOS 15 or later |
| Android phone / tablet | Android cloud app | Android 6.0 or later |
| Phone browser | Not listed as a supported browser-play method | The notice restricts the web service to computers; check the mobile cloud app instead |

The announcement labels the web service as computer-only and then lists both Windows and macOS browsers. That supports the Mac browser route described here. It does not announce a native Mac installer or show that the Windows launcher runs natively on macOS. Japan's Windows download instructions remain separate in the [official download guide](/en/guides/download/).

## Is Azur Promilia cloud gaming free?

The announcement describes a free-time allowance with a limit, rather than unlimited free streaming:

| Condition | Announced allowance | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First cloud login | Claim 600 free minutes | Equivalent to 10 hours of time allowance |
| Daily cloud login | Receive 15 free minutes | The notice does not specify the daily reset clock or time zone |
| Accumulated free-time cap | 600 minutes | Free time cannot continue accumulating once the cap is reached |

For an arithmetic example, suppose the balance before a daily grant is 590 minutes and nothing else changes. A 15-minute grant can increase that balance by at most 10 minutes under the 600-minute cap. A balance already at 600 cannot grow to 615 through the daily grant. This is a calculation from the published cap, not an observed account credit or a test of billing order.

The notice does not fully explain queue-time deductions, disconnections, the order in which free and paid benefits are consumed, balance expiry or every paid rate. Check the service's current terms before using those details to plan a session. The [cloud-session record sheet](/downloads/cloud-gaming-en.txt) helps record your balance before and after play without assuming an unverified charging rule.

## Before using cloud gaming for a storage problem

1. **Check the game region and account.** These links belong to the mainland China official service. A Japan Yostar ID, a channel-edition character and an overseas store account do not become compatible simply because the game title matches. The notice does not establish Japan–China transfers or account eligibility for every reader.
2. **Choose the correct download section.** The official page lists the regular game client and the cloud apps separately. On a phone, check the 云·蓝色星原 heading before following a button. On a computer, use the official browser route. A regular game installer is not the small cloud app.
3. **Keep cloud and native requirements separate.** The cloud notice's iOS 15 and Android 6.0 conditions do not replace the native game's CPU, GPU, RAM or storage requirements. Use the [requirements and storage guide](/en/guides/requirements/) when installing the full game. The notice calls the cloud package small but gives no verified exact download size in MB or final space usage.
4. **Read queue and network messages.** The publisher warns that peak-time queues can be long and suggests playing outside busy periods. Its description of high quality, high frame rates and low latency is a product statement; we have not measured a guaranteed frame rate, network delay or data usage. Check the experience on your own connection before planning a long session.
5. **Record balances and errors.** Note the device, OS, browser version, time, queue message and exact error. Keep first-login time, daily grants and play-card benefits separate. If you need help, use the [regional support guide](/en/guides/support/) to identify the right official route and prepare the relevant details.

## Cloud gaming FAQ

### Is there now a native Mac version?

The evidence here is for the China cloud service in a macOS browser. It does not announce a native Mac app, establish compatibility with the Windows installer or confirm a Japan Mac service. Follow the official cloud link only after checking its regional account conditions.

### Do I get ten free hours every day?

No. The notice describes 600 minutes at first cloud login, 15 minutes for daily login and a 600-minute cap on accumulated free time. It does not promise an automatic daily reset to ten hours or unlimited free access. Check your actual balance in the cloud service.

### Can I use the browser version on my phone?

The announcement marks the browser service as computer-only and lists separate Android and iOS cloud apps. A page opening on a phone does not prove that browser streaming is supported. Use the official download page to check the relevant mobile cloud app rather than inferring support from page access.

### Does the launch announcement guarantee an immediate login?

No. The publisher warns of peak-time queues. Check the current service notice and your queue or error message before retrying. A cloud queue does not call for repairing the regular game client.
